01 // What we deliver

Four data & fleet engagements, deployed in your account.Your cloud, your storage buckets, your runbooks. No recurring platform lock-in to us.

C-01 // EDGE BUFFERING & INGEST

Capture architecture

Triggered on-robot ring buffers, provenance schemas, and an upload pipeline sized to your plant's actual bitrate and Wi-Fi.

Deliverables

  • ROS 2 MCAP capture schema with full calibration & policy provenance
  • On-robot ring buffer that survives network drops and triggers on faults
  • Edge privacy redaction (face/badge blurring) before frames leave site
  • Day-one hot/warm/cold retention and storage tiering policy
C-02 // MCAP TO EPISODES

Dataset pipeline

Deterministic, versioned ETL from raw operational rosbag2/MCAP logs into training-ready episode datasets (LeRobot, RLDS).

Deliverables

  • Deterministic MCAP → LeRobot / RLDS episode conversion pipeline
  • Dataset versioning so any training set regenerates from raw logs
  • Automated episode QC filter (drops stalls, e-stops, corrupt sync)
  • Searchable episode catalog indexed by task, part, and failure mode

02 // Architecture & retention rules

Two ecosystems, one conversion pipeline.Operational diagnostics and policy-training datasets require different formats—and a deterministic bridge between them.

LayerStandard formatWhat it's built forWhat breaks without it
On-robot operational logrosbag2 / MCAPAsynchronous, multi-rate sensor streams, TF trees, and fault replay.You can't diagnose why a cell faulted at 2:14 a.m.
Provenance manifestJSON / ParquetRobot ID, camera extrinsics, gripper TCP, policy SHA, operator ID.Recorded trajectories are unusable after the next calibration.
Training datasetLeRobot / RLDSTime-aligned (observation, action, reward) episodes for GPU dataloaders.Training scripts stall on raw bag parsing and mismatched timestamps.

Keep the raw, tier it cold, and budget for data over compute. Fine-tuning a generalist policy on a few hundred episodes runs overnight on a single rented GPU ($2–$4/hr). The real line items are storage, egress, and episode curation—which is why retention rules and triggered ring buffers belong in week one.

Rollout rule: versioned artifacts, canary cell, agreed cycle metric, and one-command rollback. If you cannot roll back in under a minute, you are not ready to roll out.
