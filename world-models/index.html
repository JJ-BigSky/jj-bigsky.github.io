01 // What we deliver

Four simulation engagements, calibrated against your cell.A modest simulator you have calibrated and measured beats a photorealistic one you take on faith.

W-01 // CAD & 3DGS ASSETS

Sim environment build

Collision-ready simulation environments built from your mechanical CAD or reconstructed from cell photos via 3D Gaussian splatting.

Deliverables

  • Convex-decomposed collision meshes, joints, and inertia tensors
  • Automated CAD-to-USD/URDF/MJCF pipeline that survives revisions
  • 3D Gaussian splat reconstruction of as-built cable trays & guarding
  • Solver setup in Isaac Lab, MuJoCo Warp, Newton, or Gazebo
W-02 // ACTUATOR & CONTACT CALIBRATION

Randomization & sys-ID

System identification on your physical robot and a targeted domain-randomization plan grounded in measured contact mechanics.

Deliverables

  • Actuator friction, damping, and latency identification on hardware
  • Calibrated contact material and compliance parameters
  • Domain-randomization bounds for lighting, pose, and mass
  • Documented list of non-transferable dynamics requiring real data
W-03 // TRANSFER BENCHMARKING

Sim-to-real harness

Identical task definitions and scoring in simulation and on the physical cell so transfer correlation is tracked on every checkpoint.

Deliverables

  • Paired sim and real evaluation harness running the exact same task
  • Per-task transfer correlation score tracked across policy versions
  • Automated regression alerts when sim-to-real divergence widens
  • Optimal sim-vs.-real episode split recommendation for your budget
W-04 // STRESS TESTING

Rare-event coverage

Synthetic edge cases, occlusions, and fault scenarios generated and tested in parallel simulation before staging on steel.

Deliverables

  • Scenario catalog derived from your line fault log and FMEA
  • Thousands of randomized occlusion, lighting, and slip rollouts
  • Quantitative failure-mode coverage report per policy checkpoint
  • Shortlist of high-risk edge cases to stage safely on the physical cell

02 // Architecture & transfer guide

Three things are called “world models.”Physics simulators get the training budget today. Video and latent models augment coverage and planning.

W · 1 // PRODUCTION WORKHORSE

Physics simulators

Where most of your budget belongs.

GPU-parallel rigid and deformable solvers (MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Newton) stepping thousands of environments at once. Mature, fast, and responsible for nearly every sim-to-real policy running in production today.

W · 2 // VISUAL AUGMENTATION

Video world models

Plausible pixels, not contact forces.

Generative models that synthesize interactive video rollouts from text or a seed frame. Valuable for visual domain randomization and rare-event synthesis, but pixel prediction does not model friction or stiction.

W · 3 // COUNTERFACTUAL PLANNING

Latent world models

Learned dynamics in latent space.

Models that learn compact state transitions so a policy can plan ahead and recover from out-of-distribution states. High potential for sample efficiency; still early for sub-millimeter industrial assembly.

The transfer gap is task-shaped and predictable before you build. Locomotion, navigation, and rigid pick-and-place transfer 60%–85% from calibrated physics simulation. Contact-rich insertion, deformable cable routing, and vacuum grip on dusty surfaces require real-world demonstrations for the last two centimeters.

Solver rule: pick between MuJoCo, Isaac Lab, Gazebo, and Newton based on the contact regime you have to transfer, then measure sim-to-real correlation on every checkpoint.
