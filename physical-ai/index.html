01 // What we deliver

Four engagements when a learned policy is right.And an honest mechanical fixture recommendation when it isn't.

P-01 // FEASIBILITY & ROI

Task triage

We score your candidate tasks on part variation, contact complexity, cycle time, and demonstration cost before you commit a year.

Deliverables

  • Every candidate task scored on variation, tolerance, and payback
  • The fixture-first list: tasks cheaper to solve with hard automation
  • The learned-policy shortlist with a demonstration & compute budget
  • Architecture recommendation (VLA fine-tune, diffusion policy, or classical)
P-02 // COLLECTION & QC

Data & teleop rigs

Leader-follower or VR teleoperation rigs, operator collection protocols, and automated per-episode quality control.

Deliverables

  • Teleop rig matched to your manipulator and gripper kinematics
  • Operator collection protocol with usable-take rate tracking
  • Automated per-episode QC that rejects stalls and bad trajectories
  • Versioned LeRobot / RLDS episode schema with full provenance
P-03 // BAKE-OFFS & BENCHMARKS

Evaluation harness

Closed-loop bench testing with confidence intervals, out-of-distribution probes, and model-agnostic bake-offs.

Deliverables

  • Statistically powered success-rate benchmarks on your real parts
  • Out-of-distribution test pass (lighting shifts, novel poses, distractors)
  • Automated regression suite for new checkpoints and quantized builds
  • Head-to-head bake-off across open-weight and vendor policies
P-04 // SAFETY ARCHITECTURE

Fallbacks & interlocks

Deterministic hardware safety envelopes and recovery states around learned policies, aligned with ISO 10218:2025 and EU 2023/1230.

Deliverables

  • Hardware-rated safety envelope independent of neural network weights
  • Policy uncertainty thresholds and deterministic recovery states
  • Compliance mapping for ISO 10218:2025 and EU Machinery Reg 2023/1230
  • Safety validation test report ready for your assessor

02 // Triage & field numbers

Where a learned policy earns its place.And where a mechanical fixture and a limit switch still win.

A learned policy is probably rightA fixture is probably right
High part variation you can't fixture awaySame part, same pose, every cycle
Contact-rich assembly with tolerance for feelPosition-repeatable moves inside 0.1 mm
Deformables: cloth, cable, foam, foodRigid parts arriving on a known datum
Long tail of variants that would need 40 programsFour variants and a tool changer
You already have a data pipeline and evaluationYou have neither, and a deadline
50 – 200 DEMOS / TASK

Demonstration cost

Imitation-learning policies need 50–200 clean teleoperated episodes per task variant. A 20-task cell means 1,000–4,000 episodes plus QC.

95% – 99% UPTIME

The production gap

Factory lines expect shift-long reliability. Closing the gap between an 85% demo reel and a 99% cell is fixturing, verification, and recovery logic.

ISO 10218:2025 & EU 2023/1230

AI in safety functions

Keep learned policies inside a deterministic, hardware-rated safety envelope so model updates don't trigger high-risk recertification.
