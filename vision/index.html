01 // What we deliver

Four ways we make the robot see.Hire us for a single bench test or the full commissioned ROS 2 perception pipeline.

V-01 // OPTICS & ILLUMINATION

Sensor & lighting design

Bench test on your actual production parts, including the shiny or transparent ones that break off-the-shelf depth cameras.

Deliverables

  • Bench test report on your real parts, shiny ones included
  • Scored camera shortlist (2D, stereo, structured light, ToF, line-scan)
  • Lighting geometry, wavelength, strobing, and shrouding spec
  • Go/no-go recommendation before anyone signs a camera PO
V-02 // GEOMETRY & DRIFT

Calibration monitoring

Repeatable hand-eye and intrinsic calibration, plus an automated check that flags a bumped mount before a crash does.

Deliverables

  • Hand-eye and intrinsic calibration procedure your techs can run
  • Shop-hardened fiducial targets and mounting fixtures
  • Automated per-shift drift check that alerts on mount movement
  • Recalibration runbook with quantitative pass/fail tolerances
V-03 // ROS 2 PIPELINE

Perception pipeline build

Detect → segment → depth → pose → grasp → verify, wired into ROS 2 with measured per-stage p99.9 latency budgets.

Deliverables

  • Six modular stages in ROS 2, each benchmarked at p99.9
  • Zero-shot or CAD-based 6-DoF pose estimation for cluttered bins
  • Documented failure taxonomy and safe reject/re-try states
  • Labeled regression dataset built from your production parts
V-04 // QUALITY & RECOVERY

Verification systems

Independent inspection of the robot's grasp and placement so the cell catches slips and mis-picks without stopping the line.

Deliverables

  • Secondary verification camera checking grasp pose and placement
  • Automated re-grasp or reject-chute routing on failed picks
  • Drift and confidence telemetry that pages before quality drops
  • Timestamped inspection audit trail for your QA team

02 // Architecture & sensor guide

Six stages, and which camera fits.Four stages got much easier with foundation models. Grasp physics and verification still win or lose the cell.

  1. 01

    Detect

    Largely solved zero-shot. Open-vocabulary detectors find your part without custom training.

    Got easier
  2. 02

    Segment

    Largely solved zero-shot. Promptable segmentation gives clean masks on unseen parts.

    Got easier
  3. 03

    Depth

    Monocular foundation models are strong, but metrology still requires active stereo or structured light.

    Got easier
  4. 04

    Pose

    Zero-shot 6-DoF from CAD or reference views now works reliably in cluttered bins.

    Got easier
  5. 05

    Grasp

    Still hard. Geometry proposes; gripper compliance, friction, and part physics dispose.

    Still hard
  6. 06

    Verify

    Still skipped. The camera that checks the robot's work is worth more than the one that guides it.

    Still skipped

SensorPick this whenWatch out for
2D cameraPresence, in-plane orientation, reading marks, checking the robot's work against a known datum.No depth. Anything that needs height, tilt, or a stack.
Active / passive stereoGeneral-purpose depth on textured parts at modest accuracy. Fast, rugged, forgiving.Specular steel, clear plastic, or repetitive geometry.
Structured lightSub-millimeter 3D depth on static or slow scenes: bin picking, high-precision inspection.Direct sunlight from bay doors, shiny parts, motion blur.
Time-of-flight (ToF)Fast, longer-range depth for AMR navigation, palletizing, and coarse picking.Multi-path errors near bin corners; millimeter accuracy, not tenths.
Line-scanContinuous high-speed inspection of moving webs, extrusions, and conveyors.Requires constant motion and hardware encoder synchronization.
