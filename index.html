    01 // The loop

    Five stations, one closed loop.From camera exposure to motor torque, fleet telemetry, and back into simulation.

    Fig. 02Click any station to inspect our deliverables and run its scoping calculator.

    You can buy a robot arm in an afternoon. Commissioning the closed loop around it is the real work: seeing the part, running the policy inside a 10 ms control period, actuating safely in the physical cell, logging every episode, and stress-testing edge cases in simulation.

    We sell two things: physical cell buildout (layout, hardware bake-offs, ROS 2, and safety sign-off) and closed-loop autonomy (perception, on-robot inference, simulation, and data infrastructure).

    Hire us for one station or the whole loop—we tell you up front which pieces your line doesn't need yet.

    S-02

    Robot Selection & Integration

    Arms, cobots, AMRs, grippers, and cameras. We run bench bake-offs on your parts and integrate what wins.

    Deliverables

    • Requirements matrix and scored hardware shortlist
    • Hands-on bake-offs with your actual production parts
    • End-of-arm tooling and mechanical fixturing design
    • Site commissioning and factory acceptance tests
    S-03

    Software & Simulation

    Production ROS 2 architecture, digital twins, and hardware-in-the-loop CI that your team can maintain.

    Deliverables

    • Versioned ROS 2 workspace, nodes, and launch stack
    • Sim-first workflows (Isaac Lab, Gazebo, MuJoCo)
    • Data pipelines for MCAP logs, datasets, and evals
    • Automated hardware-in-the-loop regression rig
    S-04

    Safety & Compliance

    Task-based risk assessments, ISO 10218:2025 / ISO/TS 15066 validation, and rated e-stop architecture.
